v5: direction

Direction marks place fixed-size bearing glyphs at Cartesian or polar positions. Bearings are degrees clockwise from north and represent where wind comes from, so each glyph points in the opposite direction. Increase every to thin dense series.

Public API

direction({
  x: 'time', y: 'speed',
  xValues: times, yValues: speeds,
  directionValues: bearings, // degrees clockwise from north
  size: 14, every: 1,
})
